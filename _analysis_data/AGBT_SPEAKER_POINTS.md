# Title

## Challenging the WGS 30x status quo....

> _(was)_ `Challenging the 30x status quo with a genome quality vector: A metric-driven, technology-agnostic specification for whole-genome quality, accuracy,and cost.`

## Key Takeaways (overt)

1. Definining WGS `(quality)` by depth of coverage is increasingly uninformative.
2. A multi-dimensional vector of metrics is needed to fully describe WGS quality.
3. Depending on your application, this vector of metrics will vary significantly.
4. We advocate for an application-centric and platform-agnostic approach to defining WGS quality, where ROI, diagnostic yield, total cost, and metrics not tied to a vendor or technology are made first class considerations.
5. We present a case study of HG003 using sequencing data from 5 platforms, in hybrid combinations and applying a cost, time to result and ROI perspective in comparison to '30x'.

> Moving away from 30x as a sole specification of WGS quality will yield more `meaningful/useful` data at lower cost. Further, we take a step in the direction of making the comparison of datasets far less opaque.
